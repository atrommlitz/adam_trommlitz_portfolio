## Overview

As part of the Sandbox admission process, I collaborated with two engineers to identify and solve a real-world problem through software development. We recognized a common pain point among students: the frustration of encountering non-functional coupon codes on websites. Our solution was to create Promigo, a platform that would aggregate verified influencer coupon codes and take a percentage of resulting revenue, ensuring users access to working promotional offers.

## Key Projects

### **Market Research and Validation**

- Conducted comprehensive research on the influencer coupon code ecosystem to understand acquisition processes and company requirements
- Analyzed existing platforms and identified gaps in the current coupon code market
- Researched successful influencer partnerships and revenue-sharing models in the industry

### **Customer Discovery and Validation**

- Reached out to diverse influencers across multiple platforms to validate the business concept
- Conducted interviews to understand influencer pain points and willingness to participate in the platform
- Gathered feedback on revenue-sharing expectations and partnership requirements

### **Competitive Analysis and Market Positioning**

- Engaged with other students working on similar projects to understand their approaches and learnings
- Analyzed competitive solutions and identified unique value propositions for Promigo
- Conducted user research with target demographic to validate product-market fit

### **Strategic Planning and Presentation**

- Developed comprehensive business plan outlining the problem, solution, value proposition, and go-to-market strategy
- Created compelling presentation materials for Sandbox leadership review
- Articulated clear success metrics and implementation roadmap for the platform

## Challenges and Learnings

The primary challenge involved establishing meaningful connections with influencers in a crowded digital landscape. We discovered that personal connections and warm introductions were significantly more effective than cold outreach through social media platforms. Additionally, we learned to implement strategic messaging approaches to avoid rate limiting while maintaining professional communication standards.

## Outcome

Our comprehensive approach and well-researched business model impressed the Sandbox leadership team, resulting in successful admission to the Sandbox 05 cohort for the 2025-2026 academic year. This achievement validates the viability of our concept and provides a foundation for future development of the Promigo platform.
