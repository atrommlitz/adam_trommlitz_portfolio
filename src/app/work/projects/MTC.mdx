## Overview

From July 2021 to April 2024, I served as a Teacher and Training Supervisor at the Missionary Training Center (MTC) for Brigham Young University. In this dual role, I was responsible for both direct instruction and supervisory leadership of a team of 7 teachers, focusing on developing effective teaching methodologies and optimizing operational processes.

## Key Projects

### **Training Program Development and Implementation**

- **Curriculum Design**: Created comprehensive training meetings and instructional tools focused on building essential skills including teaching methodologies, conflict resolution, and time management
- **Performance Improvement**: Led initiatives that resulted in a 10% improvement in student-teacher evaluations across the team
- **Team Leadership**: Managed and mentored a team of 7 teachers, providing ongoing coaching and feedback to enhance instructional quality
- **Skill Development**: Designed targeted workshops to address common teaching challenges and improve team effectiveness

### **Student Conferencing System Optimization**

- **Process Redesign**: Identified inefficiencies in the student conferencing call organization system
- **Implementation**: Developed and implemented a new organizational system to streamline student conferencing processes
- **Efficiency Gains**: Achieved a 50% reduction in student wait time through strategic scheduling and resource allocation
- **User Experience**: Enhanced student satisfaction by minimizing delays and improving access to support resources

### **Leadership Excellence and Team Management**

- **Communication**: Maintained clear and consistent communication channels with teachers and students
- **Employee Satisfaction**: Fostered a positive team environment through supportive leadership and recognition
- **Performance Metrics**: Achieved an exceptional average survey rating of 4.9/5.0 based on communication, leadership, and employee satisfaction criteria
- **Continuous Improvement**: Regularly solicited feedback from team members and implemented suggestions to enhance team performance

## Challenges and Learnings

One of the primary challenges was balancing direct teaching responsibilities with supervisory duties while maintaining high standards across both roles. I learned the importance of delegation, clear communication, and creating systems that could operate efficiently without constant oversight. This experience taught me how to develop scalable processes and empower team members to take ownership of their responsibilities.

Additionally, addressing the diverse learning needs of students while managing a team required developing strong organizational and time management skills. I discovered that investing time in proper training and system development upfront pays significant dividends in long-term efficiency and team satisfaction.

## Outcome

The combination of improved training programs, optimized operational systems, and strong team leadership resulted in measurable improvements across all key metrics. The 10% increase in student-teacher evaluations demonstrated the effectiveness of our training initiatives, while the 50% reduction in student wait times validated our process improvements. Most importantly, the 4.9/5.0 leadership satisfaction rating reflected the positive team culture and effective management practices that were established during my tenure.
