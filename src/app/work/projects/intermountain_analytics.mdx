## Overview

During my junior year internship at Intermountain Healthcare, I developed 10+ strategic dashboards valued at over $800,000 to optimize operational efficiency for Nurse and Medical Assistant Residency programs and Simulation departments.

## Key Projects

### **Nurse Residency Dashboard Performance Optimization**

- **Performance Improvement**: Reduced dashboard query execution time from 5 minutes to under 2 minutes by optimizing SQL queries
- **Technical Enhancements**: Replaced inefficient `IN` and `NOT IN` statements with optimized `LEFT JOIN` and `INNER JOIN` operations
- **Advanced Analytics**: Leveraged window functions to implement new dashboard functionality and improve data insights

### **Nurse Residency KPI Dashboard Development**

- **Feedback Management**: Created comprehensive dashboards to surface nurse feedback for management decision-making
- **Retention Tracking**: Implemented systems to monitor nurse retention rates post-residency program completion
- **Process Automation**: Automated key Excel workflows in Tableau to streamline manager reporting and area tracking

### **Tableau Competition Achievement - 3rd Place**

- **Competitive Success**: Competed against managers and directors as part of an intern team
- **Data Visualization**: Developed an innovative dashboard analyzing National Parks Species Dataset to track endangered and threatened species
- **Team Collaboration**: Coordinated with teammates to conceptualize and execute our strategic vision

## Challenges and Learnings

The primary challenge involved implementing advanced Tableau features without prior experience. I overcame this by leveraging online resources, including YouTube tutorials, to master techniques like creating lollipop charts with custom images for enhanced visual appeal.

## Outcome

Our team's innovative approach and collaborative effort resulted in a 3rd place finish, demonstrating our technical capabilities and problem-solving skills.
