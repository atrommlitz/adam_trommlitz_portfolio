## Overview

During my summer internship, I led a comprehensive project focused on enhancing the canvas tagging experience within Lucid. I was given the opportunity to take ownership of this initiative from initial research through final recommendations, providing me with valuable end-to-end product management experience.

## Key Projects

### **Goal Setting and Success Metrics**

- Conducted thorough analysis of existing changes and established clear success criteria for the project
- Identified key performance indicators to measure project success and user experience improvements
- Utilized SQL queries to establish baseline analytics for the current tagging experience, enabling data-driven decision making

### **Internal User Research**

- Conducted comprehensive interviews with internal stakeholders to understand their use cases and pain points with the current tagging system
- Leveraged NotebookLM to analyze interview transcripts and generate structured insights including use cases, identified problems, and potential solutions
- Discovered previously unknown use cases through user research, leading to innovative solutions developed in collaboration with UX designers
- Partnered with Solution Engineers to gather customer feedback and understand common frustrations with the tagging experience

### **External Customer Research**

- Developed and distributed targeted email campaigns to schedule interviews with power users and enterprise customers
- Gathered valuable insights from high-value accounts regarding their tagging use cases and critical pain points

### **Strategic Prioritization**

- Applied MSCW and RICE frameworks to systematically prioritize problems and solutions
- Developed prioritization criteria based on use case impact and the number of affected users
- Ensured resource allocation aligned with maximum customer and business value

### **Data Analytics and Insights**

- Utilized Snowflake and SQL to analyze tag usage patterns and identify friction points in the user journey
- Discovered significant usage spikes from newer Lucid accounts, leading to strategic discussions with account teams about tag adoption opportunities
- Generated actionable insights to inform product roadmap decisions

### **Cross-functional Collaboration**

- Worked closely with UX designers to brainstorm innovative solutions and validate design concepts
- Implemented derisking strategies to ensure optimal resource allocation and project success

## Challenges and Learnings

This project presented a significant learning opportunity as my first foray into product management. I gained valuable experience in derisking strategies to ensure our team focused resources on the most impactful improvements for both customers and the business. Throughout the project, I received exceptional mentorship and guidance on project management best practices and success measurement methodologies.

## Outcome

The project has progressed to version 0 and is currently under active development by the engineering team, with implementation based on the research findings and recommendations developed during this initiative.
